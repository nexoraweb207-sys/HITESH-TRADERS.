Sanganeri Print Care Guide

Simple practices to preserve print vibrancy and fabric softness for years.

Cold Gentle Wash

Machine wash in cold water with mild detergent to protect natural dyes.

Wash Separately

Wash dark indigo and deep red tones separately during initial washes.

Dry in Shade

Line dry inside-out under shade to maintain natural cotton luster.

Medium Iron

Iron on medium-warm heat for a smooth, hotel-quality bed feel.
